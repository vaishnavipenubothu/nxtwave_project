Demographic: Final-year B.Tech / B.E. students (CSE, IT, ECE, AI/DS, Core branches) primarily in Tier-2 and Tier-3 engineering colleges across India (JNTU, Anna Univ, VTU, SRM, AKTU).

Growth Experiments, AI Worklog & Judgment

🧪 3 A/B Growth Tests (Hypothesis ➔ Test ➔ Metric)
Test 1 (Hero Hook):
• Hypothesis: Framing the workshop as "Placement Resume Boost" will convert 35% higher than "Learn AI".
• Test: A/B test "Build Your First AI Project" vs. "Add a Real AI Project to Your Placement Resume in 60 Mins".
• Metric: Landing Page Visitor-to-Registration Rate (CVR).
Test 2 (Viral Incentive):
• Hypothesis: Instant tangible utility (Top 50 AI Prompts PDF) drives higher referral sharing than future prizes.
• Test: Generic referral link vs. Immediate 1-referral milestone unlock for AI Interview Cheat-Sheet.
• Metric: Viral Coefficient (k-factor) and WhatsApp share clicks per registrant.
Test 3 (Channel Delivery Framing):
• Hypothesis: Student peer recommendations in class groups convert 2.5x better than official placement cell notices.
• Test: Official TPO Circular copy vs. Casual CR voice note + peer invite message.
• Metric: Click-through rate (CTR) and Registration velocity per college group.
💡 AI Worklog & What I Deliberately Rejected
What AI Suggested That I Rejected:
1. Spending budget on Meta/Google Ads: AI suggested running Instagram/Facebook leads ads. With ₹2,000, ad learning phases consume budget with ₹25-40 CPL, yielding barely 60 registrations. I rejected this and diverted ₹1,200 into direct Class Representative micro-incentives, which yield high-trust 50% conversion at ₹4.58/reg.
2. Mandatory GitHub sign-in / Pre-requisite Quiz: AI suggested pre-qualifying students with a 5-question technical screening quiz. I rejected this because final-year students are overwhelmed; artificial friction destroys registration momentum.
What Changed from First Idea to Final Solution:
Started with a standard webinar landing page. Pivoted to an interactive AI Readiness & Project Matching Hub with gamified referral perks and real-time seats urgency, turning every registered student into an active recruiter.
If Given Another 24 Hours:
I would integrate an automated WhatsApp Cloud API bot to instantly ping registrants with calendar invites and trigger automated reminders 2 hours before the workshop to maximize the show-up rate.
